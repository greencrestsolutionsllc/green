Opening paragraph. States the question the article examines, the development that prompts it, and why the answer is not yet obvious. Two to four sentences, written for a general reader without simplifying the subject.

Second paragraph. Introduces the evidence the argument will rely on and the primary sources it draws from.<Fn n={1} /> Where figures appear, they are cited to the original publisher rather than to secondary coverage.

## Section heading

Body paragraph. Develops one part of the argument. Section headings are used sparingly, roughly every 600 to 900 words.
